## Flag images

The PNG files in `assets/flags/` are rendered from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) (`flags/4x3`) by `tool/generate_flags.py`. flag-icons is MIT licensed, copyright 2013 Panayiotis Lipiridis. The license is in `docs/licenses/flag-icons-MIT.txt`.

Three files with no flag-icons source (`an.png`, `cs.png`, `fam.png`) are still the originals from the [famfamfam flag distribution](https://github.com/legacy-icons/famfamfam-flags), shared with Shifter Proxy Extension and released with a public-domain dedication.

## Interface icons

The inline stroke icons in `lib/ui/widgets/icons.dart` include paths derived from [Feather](https://github.com/feathericons/feather). Feather is MIT licensed, copyright 2013–2023 Cole Bemis. The license is reproduced below and in `docs/licenses/feather-MIT.txt`. Additional Shifter-specific icon paths are covered by the project license.

## Packet tunnel engine

Full-device mode on iOS and Android uses [hev-socks5-tunnel](https://github.com/heiher/hev-socks5-tunnel), built from a pinned commit into `ios/Frameworks/HevSocks5Tunnel.xcframework` and `android/app/src/main/jniLibs/` by `tool/ios/build_hev_xcframework.sh` and `tool/android/build_hev_jni.sh`. It bundles three libraries:

- hev-socks5-tunnel and [hev-task-system](https://github.com/heiher/hev-task-system): MIT licensed, copyright 2022 hev.
- [lwIP](https://savannah.nongnu.org/projects/lwip/): BSD licensed, copyright 2001, 2002 Swedish Institute of Computer Science.
- [libyaml](https://github.com/yaml/libyaml), through a fork by hev: MIT licensed, copyright 2006–2016 Kirill Simonov, 2017–2020 Ingy döt Net, 2022 hev.

The licenses are reproduced below, in `docs/licenses/`, and next to the built engine on both platforms.
